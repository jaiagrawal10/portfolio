01 / About

Curious by nature. Analytical by craft.

I’ve always enjoyed figuring out how things work — and over the years, that curiosity has naturally led me toward data, technology, and problem-solving.

I’m a Data & Analytics professional with 4+ years of experience working across data analytics, data engineering, and information security. Today, I work as a Data Analyst at Flipkart, where I work with 3+ lakh records daily as part of Last Mile Operations, build Power BI dashboards, and use Python to automate reporting and make information easier for teams to access and act on.

What I enjoy most about analytics isn’t just writing a query or building a dashboard. It’s taking something complicated — a large dataset, a repetitive reporting process, or a business problem — and turning it into something simple, useful, and actionable.

My journey has also given me a perspective beyond traditional analytics. Earlier in my career at Optum and Valuemomentum, I worked with SQL, log analysis, cybersecurity data, access reviews, reporting, and process improvement. Those experiences taught me to look at data not just as numbers, but as a way to understand how systems and businesses actually operate.

Today, my toolkit includes:

  • SQL and Python for analysis and automation
  • Power BI for dashboards and data storytelling
  • Advanced Excel for analysis and reporting
  • Pandas, NumPy and EDA for working with complex datasets
  • AI tools such as Claude Code for building practical solutions and simplifying workflows

I’m particularly interested in the intersection of data, automation, and AI — especially where technology can eliminate repetitive work, improve visibility, and help people make better decisions.

I’m still learning, experimenting, and building. And that’s probably the part of working with data I enjoy the most: there’s always another problem to solve and another better way to solve it.

Always happy to connect with people working in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Data Engineering, and AI.
